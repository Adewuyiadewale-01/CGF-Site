[ 09 ]  Questions

Good questions, plain answers.

Still curious? Talk to a person in Sydney, weekdays 8 to 6.

Do I need a home battery?

No. The street battery does the storing. If you already have one, we pair it with the network so it earns too.

What happens in a blackout?

The street battery is grid-forming: if the network drops, it can island your street and keep essential circuits running for hours.

Who owns the street battery?

Nanomantic builds and maintains it, on land agreed with council and the local network. Members share in what it earns.

Can I leave whenever I want?

Yes. There’s no lock-in contract. Leave with 30 days’ notice and keep every credit you’ve earned.

I rent. Can I still join?

Yes. Renters join for the evening savings, and landlords with panels can join for the shared-solar credit.

How do I get paid?

Credits are worked out every half hour and land on your power bill each month. You can watch them build in the app.
